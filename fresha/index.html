“Spotlighting individuality to refresh beauty and wellness”

One of the world’s leading booking platforms for beauty and wellness, Fresha needed a new brand that would prepare them for future growth. Working at DesignStudio, I worked on every aspect of artwork from the main logos to print tests. This included working with the design team to create a robust version of the complex gradient symbol that would print well on merchandise such as t-shirts.







The creation of a set of icons was undertaken with precision to be able to be used across many touch points and in motion.
The rebrand for Fresha launched in early 2024.
