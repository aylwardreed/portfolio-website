“Placing community at the centre of money transfer”

Creating a fresh and exciting visual identity for this popular money transfer platform, I led artworking duties on the creation of logos, icons and large scale static ‘wave’ imagery. Extensive print tests were needed to get the right colours for print which I oversaw.







An exciting project that was tough but ultimately succesful in creating, the new look for Sendwave launched in early 2024.
