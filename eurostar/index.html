“Merging rail giants with a spark”

DesignStudio was tasked with full rebrand and creation of a design system for Eurostar as it merged with Thalys to create a larger, more wide reaching rail service. Working with the design team I led the artwork refinement and file creation of the core logos, icons, graphic devices and more. Additionally, I created many example applications using the brand photography. The dynamic, flexible, and colourful rebrand launched at the end of 2023.







Following on from the success for the core rebrand, DesignStudio was asked to expand the new design style to ‘Snap’, Eurostar’s platform for cheaper, last-minute tickets. Creating several hundred assets in various formats, I worked on the creation of the main logo and the location stamps.
