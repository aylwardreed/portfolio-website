“Unleashing a new era for esports”

Starting with the European league, DesignStudio branded four regional leagues and created many subsequent designs for yearly finals for the professional League of Legends competitions. Covering many assets from Logos to icons to guidelines to full poster layouts, I took the lead on artwork for every project.







As well as exploring colour values through print tests I also created automated Photoshop templates for the client to use and avoid navigating complex layer effects and filters.




Full case studies on the DesignStudio website. EU, China, Korea, and Latin America.
