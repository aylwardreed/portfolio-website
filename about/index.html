HI, I’M JONATHAN, A SENIOR
CREATIVE ARTWORKER WITH 25 YEARS EXPERIENCE WORKING ON SOME OF
THE WORLD’S BIGGEST BRANDS
I have led artwork teams both in-house (at Molton Brown) and agency-side (at DesignStudio). My role has encompassed everything from working with design teams to press approval to crafting precise and distinctive logos and icons to creating powerful automation workflows that save hours of project time.

At the heart of my approach lies a meticulous eye for design details and typography and a comprehensive understanding of print and production processes, digital formats, and the latest technological advances. Leveraging my proficiency in software such as Adobe Creative Suite, my aim is always to deliver creative and technically sound solutions that uphold the visual standards of design teams while providing assets for clients in fast and accurate ways.
